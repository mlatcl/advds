# CIP-0001: Advanced Data Science Course Tenets

## Summary

This CIP establishes core tenets for the Advanced Data Science course, providing guiding principles for students to understand the data science process and apply best practices in their work.

## Motivation

The Advanced Data Science course introduces students to the emerging domain of data science and guides them through the data science pipeline. Clear tenets will help students navigate the course material and understand the fundamental principles of data science practice.

## Detailed Description

The tenets embody the core principles of the Advanced Data Science course, focusing on the Fynesse framework (Access, Assess, Address) and key data science approaches like "data science as debugging." These tenets will serve as guiding principles for students throughout their learning journey.

### Tenet 1: The Fynesse Framework

**Access → Assess → Address**: The data science process follows a clear progression from accessing data, to assessing its properties, to addressing the questions of interest. This framework provides a structured approach to data science problems.

### Tenet 2: Data Science as Debugging

Approach data analysis with the mindset of a debugger - be constantly skeptical, understand the landscape of your data, try different approaches, and document your process meticulously.

### Tenet 3: Disciplined Integration

Data science sits at the intersection of statistics, machine learning, and domain expertise. Successful data science requires integrating these disciplines while maintaining scientific rigor.

### Tenet 4: Continuous Learning and Adaptation

The data science process is inherently iterative. Continuous refinement of approaches based on findings is essential, similar to the "Experiment, Analyze, Design" flywheel.

### Tenet 5: Collaborative Workflows

Data scientists should not work in isolation. Effective data science requires collaboration with domain experts, software engineers, and stakeholders to deliver robust solutions.

### Tenet 6: Ethical Data Handling

Data science carries responsibility. Practice ethical collection, analysis, and application of data, being mindful of bias, privacy, and potential societal impacts.

### Tenet 7: Data Quality Awareness

Be vigilant about data quality issues. Data is rarely clean, and understanding limitations and biases in the data is as important as the analysis itself.

## Implementation Plan

1. **Documentation**:
   - Add these tenets to course documentation
   - Reference tenets in lecture materials

2. **Integration into Teaching**:
   - Highlight relevant tenets during lectures
   - Design practical exercises that reinforce these principles

3. **Assessment Alignment**:
   - Ensure course assessments reflect these core tenets
   - Provide feedback based on adherence to these principles

## Backward Compatibility

These tenets formalize principles already present in the course material but provide a more structured framework for students to understand the key aspects of data science practice.

## Testing Strategy

Effectiveness of these tenets will be measured through:
- Student feedback surveys
- Quality of student project work
- Ability of students to articulate data science principles
